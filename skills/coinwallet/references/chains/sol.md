Rent: every Solana address must keep at least 0.00089 SOL.
- SOL to a new address: less than 0.00089 SOL fails. Ask for 0.001 SOL or more.
- Sending the whole SOL balance fails: leave 0.001 SOL.
- A token (USDT, USDC) to an address that never held it: you pay about 0.00204 SOL to open its token account. Say so in the Send check.
Memo: the user said it's their own wallet → no memo, don't ask. Else, no memo given → before the Send check, ask: "Is this an exchange or shared address? If yes, paste the memo. If it's your own wallet, say 'no memo'." Send it as `memo`. Send check: "Memo: <memo>" or "Memo: no memo".
